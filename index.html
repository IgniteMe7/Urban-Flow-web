Engineering the Self-Healing City

South Africa loses 47% of treated municipal water to infrastructure failure. Urban Flow is an anticipatory mechatronic framework that converts passive pipelines into an intelligent, self-healing network. Our dual-sensor ecosystem—autonomous isolation nodes and sub-surface diagnostics—enables real-time rupture detection, instantaneous containment, and surgical precision repairs. The result: municipal water systems that learn, adapt, and heal themselves.

Request Technical Brief

🚰 Anticipatory Isolation

Autonomous valve nodes detect pressure drops and isolate ruptures in under 8 seconds. We stop floods before they start, protecting informal settlement infrastructure and minimizing water loss in critical urban corridors.

🤖 Precision Diagnostics

Our sub-surface crawler pinpoints hidden leaks with ±10 cm accuracy. We enable surgical, cost-effective repairs without disruptive municipal excavations, saving time, money, and community disruption.

👩🏽‍💻 Inclusive Workforce

We train young women and men as certified technical operators. By 2027, 50% of our field maintenance pipeline will be female-led, bridging the gender gap in South African STEM careers and building local economic capacity.
