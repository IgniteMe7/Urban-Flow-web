Engineering the Self-Healing City

South Africa loses 47% of treated municipal water to infrastructure failure. Urban Flow is an anticipatory mechatronic framework that converts passive pipelines into an active smart grid—safeguarding marginalised communities and conserving our most precious resource.

Request Technical Brief

🚰 Anticipatory Isolation

Autonomous valve nodes detect pressure drops and isolate ruptures in under 8 seconds. We stop floods before they start, protecting informal settlement infrastructure.

🤖 Precision Diagnostics

Our sub-surface crawler pinpoints hidden leaks with ±10 cm accuracy. We enable surgical, cost-effective repairs without disruptive municipal excavations.

👩🏽‍💻 Inclusive Workforce

We train young women and men as certified technical operators. By 2027, 50% of our field maintenance pipeline will be female-led, bridging the gender gap in South African STEM.
